Cloud Cover
Cloud Cover page

Live satellite picture of cloud cover around your location. Updates every 10 to 15 minutes at the source; the newest frame is usually 30 to 45 minutes old. Location comes from the System tab's location settings.

The page works anywhere in the world and picks the satellite that sits over your location: GOES East and GOES West for the Americas, Himawari for Asia, Australia and New Zealand, and Meteosat for Europe, Africa, the Middle East and the Indian Ocean. There is nothing to choose; move your location and the picture follows.

Himawari and the Indian Ocean Meteosat have no picture that works day and night, so with GeoColor selected they show a daylight picture and switch to infrared after dark, frame by frame. Himawari's daylight picture is black and white; the Indian Ocean one is natural colour. Air mass over Europe and Africa comes from the older Meteosat satellite, which publishes every 15 minutes rather than every 10.

Which satellite picture to show. GeoColor looks like the view from space but only shows cloud properly in daylight; over Asia it is a black-and-white daytime picture and over the Indian Ocean a natural-colour one, both switching to infrared at night. Clean infrared shows cloud tops around the clock, so it is the one to use at night. Air mass colours air by temperature and moisture; it updates less often than the other two.

Animation
Animation

Update Interval sets how often the page checks for a new satellite picture. The source publishes a new picture every 10 minutes, so checking more often than that gains nothing.

Animation length sets how many recent pictures the page plays back. Pictures are 10 minutes apart, so 6 frames is roughly the last hour; 1 frame is a still picture that never animates. Each frame takes about 1 MB of memory, so pick a shorter animation if the device is short on room.
