Moon
Moon page

Adds a full-screen page with a 3D-rendered moon showing the current phase, drawn on the device from your location (System tab) and the current time; nothing is downloaded. Tap the moon to animate it, drag to rotate it.

Refresh Interval sets how often the view is redrawn to follow the sky. Background, lighting and the Moon Orientation card control how the disc looks and how it responds to dragging.

Moon Orientation
Moon appearance and orientation

Corrects how the rendered moon is oriented and how it responds to dragging. Keep Moon upright (north-up) holds the moon's north pole at the top like phone apps and NASA imagery; turning it off tilts the disc to match how the real moon sits in your local sky as it moves. The device location set on the System tab is used for an accurate sky-tilt and terminator.

Flip horizontal (U) and Flip vertical (V) mirror the image for displays or mounts that reverse it. The Roll, Yaw, and Pitch offset sliders nudge the moon from its standard orientation, and Reset orientation returns them to that standard view.

Free-spin mode changes drag behavior. Off, the disc rubber-bands back to the live sky as soon as you release it. On, the disc holds your spin and eases back after the Return to live view timeout (3 to 60 seconds). Example: enable Free-spin, set the return to 15s, drag to inspect the far limb, and the view holds for 15 seconds before returning to live.
