Voice clips

The device speaks alerts by playing short sound clips back to back. Every clip ships with a built-in default voice; here you can replace any of them with your own recording and hear the result on the device speaker.

Upload replaces a clip, the play button speaks the currently active version on the device, and Reset returns a clip to its built-in default. Changes take effect immediately; there is nothing to save.

Example: record your own "Warning" in your voice, upload it, then press play to hear it from the panel.

Upload any common audio format (mp3, m4a, wav, ogg); your browser converts it to the required 16 kHz mono PCM before uploading. Files ending in .pcm are sent as-is. Clips are limited to 10 seconds each; Boot Jingle may be up to 15 seconds.
