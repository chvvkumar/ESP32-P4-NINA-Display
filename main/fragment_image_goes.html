GOES Satellite
GOES satellite page

Adds a full-screen page showing the latest GOES-19 GEOCOLOR image for one sector. Pick the sector under Region; the picture refreshes at the update interval you choose.

Fill (crop borders) zooms the disc to fill the 720x720 panel and hides the timestamp and label baked into the image border. Rotation and the two flips correct a picture that appears sideways or mirrored on your panel.
