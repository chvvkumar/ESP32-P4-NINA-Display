Solar page

Adds a full-screen page showing the latest NASA SDO/AIA, SOHO or NOAA GOES SUVI solar image for one band. Pick the band; the picture refreshes at the update interval you choose.

Fill (crop borders) zooms the solar disc to fill the 720x720 panel and hides the timestamp and label baked into the image border. Rotation and the two flips correct a picture that appears sideways or mirrored on your panel.
