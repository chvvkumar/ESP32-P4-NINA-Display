ADS-B
ADS-B page

Adds a page that shows the aircraft your own ADS-B receiver is hearing, with the direction your telescopes are pointing marked on it. The data comes from a tar1090 or readsb receiver on your network, so the device needs its address.

Receiver holds the address and how often it is read. View picks how the aircraft are drawn and which way the display is turned. There is no aircraft data from the internet here, only from your own receiver.

Enable ADS-B page
Adds the ADS-B page and starts reading from your receiver; off hides the page and stops the requests.
Receiver
Receiver

Receiver Address is the address of your tar1090 or readsb page, the same one you open in a browser. Include http:// and a port number if you use one, and leave off any path.

Refresh sets how often the aircraft list is read, from 2 to 60 seconds. Three seconds keeps a fast jet moving smoothly.

Range sets how far out the Radar Scope view reaches. It does not limit what the Sky Dome or Board views show.

Look up flight routes online
Shows origin and destination (for example LAX-STL) next to each flight. Uses the free adsb.im route service over the internet; turn off to keep the page fully local.
View
View

Sky Dome draws the sky as seen looking straight up: the middle of the circle is directly overhead and the edge is the horizon. Radar Scope draws a flat map with distance from you, like a radar screen. Board is a plain list of the nearest aircraft with no picture. Tapping the page on the device also cycles through the three.

Minimum elevation hides aircraft that sit low on the horizon, where buildings and trees are in the way anyway.

Up direction turns the whole picture so that a compass direction of your choosing is at the top of the screen. Set it to the direction your house or window faces and what you see on screen matches what you see outside. Dragging on the device turns it too, and the two stay in step.

How the aircraft are drawn; a tap on the device cycles through the same three.
Shape drawn for each aircraft on the Radar Scope. Aircraft shapes pick a jet, small plane, or helicopter outline from the aircraft's reported category. Sky Dome and Board are not affected.
Which aircraft on the Radar Scope get a text label (flight, altitude and speed) next to their arrow. "Up to a number" labels the aircraft nearest to your receiver first. Sky Dome and Board are not affected.
Degrees above the horizon, 0 to 89. Aircraft lower than this are counted but not drawn.
